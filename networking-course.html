What is a computer network? Picture a digital highway system. It connects devices so they can communicate and share resources — every device is a vehicle, and the roads carry data to its destination. At home, your phone, laptop, tablet and TV all connect through one central hub — your router, the traffic controller. Zoom out and those same highways span cities, countries and continents: that is the internet. It began in 1969 as ARPANET, a small network linking four universities — and never stopped growing.
