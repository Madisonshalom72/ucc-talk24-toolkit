TOOL 02 / 07

UCC-1 Filing Checklist

A preparation sequence for a financing statement—not a substitute for the filing office’s form or instructions.

  • Debtor details
  • Secured party details
  • Collateral description
  • Filing office review

Preparation sequence

Identify the debtor exactly

Use the correct legal name and entity details. Name errors can make a filing ineffective.

Identify the secured party

Prepare the secured party’s legal name and mailing address as the official form requires.

Describe the collateral

Use a description appropriate to the security agreement and the property covered.

Choose the correct filing office

Filing location depends on the debtor and collateral. Confirm it with the applicable Secretary of State or other filing office.

Review local form rules and fees

Check the current official UCC-1 form, accepted payment method, attachments, and indexing rules.

Keep the acknowledgment

Retain the file number, filing date, image, receipt, and any later continuation or amendment deadlines.

UCC filing rules are state-specific. Verify every filing against the current instructions from the proper filing office.
